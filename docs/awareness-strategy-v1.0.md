# ResilientSA — Awareness Strategy
## Bridge Document | Version 1.0
*Records the Captain's decision on community discovery and awareness. Short by design — the decision is clear.*

---

## 1. The Governing Decision

**ResilientSA does not approach communities. Communities reach out to ResilientSA.**

This is a deliberate, permanent design choice — not a Phase 1 constraint to be revisited later. The communities ResilientSA exists to serve have been approached, promised, and disappointed by outside organisations more times than they can count. Any approach, however carefully designed, carries the residue of that history. The risk of alienation outweighs any benefit of proactive outreach.

The platform creates awareness. Communities decide. Entry into the platform is always community-initiated.

This decision is recorded here so it is not relitigated as the mission scales. If someone later proposes an active outreach programme, this document is the reference.

---

## 2. What This Means for Onboarding

Mission Brief Section 9.4 Stage 1 — "Expression of Interest: Community contacts ResilientSA" — is the only valid entry point. There is no Entry Point 2. No approach channel exists. No Elder or Grounder is authorised to recruit communities on ResilientSA's behalf.

Once a community makes contact on their own terms, the full Community Health Protocol (`docs/community-health-protocol-spec-v1.0.md`) and Onboarding Journey (Mission Brief Section 9.4) apply as specified.

## 3. The Awareness Channel Mix

Awareness creates pull. Communities who recognise themselves in what they hear, see, or read reach out on their own terms. The channels below are chosen specifically for the communities ResilientSA serves — not for general digital marketing reach.

### Channel 1 — Community Radio (Primary)

**Why:** Community radio is where trust lives in South African township and peri-urban communities. Over 200 licensed community radio stations operate nationally. SABC regional stations (Umhlobo Wenene FM for Xhosa-speaking communities, Ukhozi FM for isiZulu, RSG for Afrikaans), community stations (Bush Radio in Cape Town, Jozi FM in Johannesburg), and faith-based stations all reach the populations ResilientSA serves — in their home languages, through voices they already trust.

**Format:** Not advertising. Storytelling. A 3–5 minute community feature — "here's what a community in [relatable area] is doing together, and the infrastructure they're using" — travels further and lands differently than a service announcement. Once pilot communities exist, their own voices telling their own stories is the most credible awareness content imaginable.

**Language advantage:** Community radio operates in home languages from day one — before the platform's Afrikaans and isiZulu translations are live, radio awareness already reaches non-English speakers in their own language.

**When:** After ORDER 010 (pilot-ready). Not before — there is nothing real to point to yet, and a campaign before pilot evidence would itself feel like empty promise culture.

### Channel 2 — Facebook (Secondary)

**Why:** Most South African township RAs and CPFs already have active Facebook groups. A ResilientSA page with community stories, plain-language explanations, and — once they exist — pilot community testimonials, gets shared organically inside those groups. This is presence, not advertising.

**Format:** Community stories first. Functional explanations second. Never promotional copy. The page should read like a community noticeboard, not a brand channel.

**When:** A basic page can be created before ORDER 010 to establish presence. Active content begins once pilot evidence exists.

### Channel 3 — TikTok (Tertiary, Longer Horizon)

**Why:** The primary TikTok demographic in South Africa skews younger than the RA chairperson or Cell Steward ResilientSA is initially reaching — but this demographic is the next generation of community leadership. Short, honest, human videos of real community moments (not produced content) seeded now become the awareness channel for Phase 2 and beyond.

**Format:** Unpolished. Real. Community members speaking in their own words, in their own languages, about things that matter to them. Nothing that looks like marketing.

**When:** Phase 2 — once pilot communities have been running long enough to generate authentic moments worth sharing.

---

## 4. What Awareness Content Is Not

Consistent with the platform's founding commitments and the Captain's explicit framing of Cape Town communities as evidence-based and tired of empty promises:

- Not promotional copy about the platform's features
- Not aspirational imagery of communities that don't exist yet
- Not statistics or impact claims without real pilot evidence behind them
- Not content that requires a community to trust ResilientSA before they've seen it work
- Not targeted advertising that reaches people who didn't ask to hear about it

The awareness content earns attention. It doesn't demand it.

## 5. Uhura's Standing Awareness Scope

Per `UHURA_INTEL.md` Section 1 (Regulatory Watch), Uhura monitors the external environment for ResilientSA. The following awareness-related signals are added to Uhura's standing watch:

- Community radio programme opportunities relevant to the awareness strategy
- RA/CPF Facebook group activity referencing community resilience, mutual aid, or platform-based cooperation — signals that awareness is landing or that a community might be approaching readiness
- Any organisations or campaigns in the SA community resilience space that could be confused with ResilientSA — important for positioning clarity
