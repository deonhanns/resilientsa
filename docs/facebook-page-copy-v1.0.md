## ABOUT SECTION (Long description)
ResilientSA is a community platform rooted in Ubuntu — built for South African communities who know that looking after each other is how we survive and thrive.

We help communities share what they have — skills, food, knowledge, energy, water, health resources — through a simple gift economy. No money changes hands. You offer what you can, you ask for what you need, and the community holds it together.

Built on the structures communities already have — Resident Associations, Community Police Forums, neighbourhood networks.

Works on any phone. Works when the internet doesn't.

**For communities ready to reach out:**
📧 [contact email]
🌐 [website when live]

We don't approach communities. If this sounds like something your community needs, reach out on your own terms. No pitch. No pressure.

## WHAT NOT TO POST (standing content rule, per Awareness Strategy)
- No feature lists or platform screenshots before pilot communities exist
- No statistics or impact claims without real evidence
- No aspirational stock imagery of communities
- No promotional language — the page reads like a community noticeboard, not a brand channel
- No content that asks communities to trust ResilientSA before they've seen it work

**First real content goes live when the first pilot community has a real story to tell — in their own words, with their consent.**
