### Two-Speed Framework

**Slow signals (calibrate monthly/quarterly):** FAO Food Price Index, Fragile States Index, IMF WEO, SARB Quarterly Bulletin, SARCOF. Update Community Resilience Signal Watch when released. Used to adjust baseline confidence weighting of internal forecasts.

**Fast signals (monitor weekly minimum, immediately for critical events):** EskomSePush (automated via O'Brien), SAWS, NICD, municipal disruption notices, BER weekly, media monitoring. Stage 6 load shedding or declared outbreak triggers immediate logging regardless of cadence.
