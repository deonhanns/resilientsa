**NetworkSummary** — one plain-language sentence about how the cell's connections are doing. No graphs, no jargon — the words a person would say, with a single calm signal.

```jsx
<NetworkSummary trend="growing"
  message="More members are connecting directly with each other, not just through you."
  stat="12 new direct links this month" />
```

`trend` sets the tint/glyph (growing = Aloe, steady = Rain, thinning = Ochre). Keep `message` conversational.
