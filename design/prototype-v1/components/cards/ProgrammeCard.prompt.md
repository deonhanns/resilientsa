**ProgrammeCard** — a Community Marketplace offering. Reads like browsing for help: plain-language name, pillar served, a proof signal ("N communities used this"), and a quiet provider credit.

```jsx
<ProgrammeCard pillar="water" title="Fix a broken communal tap"
  summary="A plumber trains two people from your area to repair and maintain shared taps."
  communitiesCount={38} provider="Cape Water Collective" onRequest={...} />
```

Keep `summary` jargon-free. The provider is deliberately secondary — the help comes first.
