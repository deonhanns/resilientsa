**EmptyState** — the calm placeholder when a list is empty. Frames absence as growth (sprout glyph), never as failure.

```jsx
<EmptyState
  title="Nothing here yet"
  body="Be the first to offer something to your cell."
  action={<Button icon="plus">Add a listing</Button>}
/>
```

Swap `icon` for context. Keep the copy warm and forward-looking.
